Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

OBS YouTube Helper is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Zedra19 YouTube channel on their own computer. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses

  • Your YouTube channel, videos and live broadcasts (to read them and change titles, descriptions, tags, category, privacy and live chat messages when you ask it to).
  • Your YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, subscribers, concurrent viewers).

How data is used and stored

  • Data is shown to you on your own computer and used only to carry out the commands you run.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on your computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google's APIs.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

You can revoke the tool's access at any time at Google security settings. Deleting the local token file removes all stored data.
